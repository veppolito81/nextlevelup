## What to expect from a pickup

- **Clear communication:** Crews provide upfront, volume-based pricing before anything goes on the truck.
- **Full-service loading:** Team members lift, load, and sweep up so you do not have to move heavy items or rent equipment.
- **Responsible disposal:** Whenever possible, items are donated or recycled instead of heading straight to the landfill.

## Tips to speed up your appointment

1. **Sort by area.** Group items by room or project (garage, office, yard) so crews can load quickly.
2. **Make a quick list.** Note special items like appliances or construction debris that may need extra care.
3. **Clear pathways.** Open driveways, stairwells, and gates to keep everyone safe and reduce time on site.
4. **Ask about reuse.** Let the team know if you have donations so they can separate them during loading.
